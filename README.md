# Commercial Legal Risk Project
Independent project exploring how legal risk affects commercial decision-making, with a focus on practical, business-focused legal analysis.


## Aims 

- Through a series of simulated in-house legal exercises, the project aims to demonstrate a practical and commercially focused approach to legal analysis, including risk assessment, contract review, executive communication and incident response.
- The focus is not simply on identifying legal risk, but on assessing how that risk interacts with operational priorities, financial exposure, reputation and wider business strategy.

## Project Areas

- Commercial legal risk assessment
- Contract and supplier risk
- Data protection and regulatory considerations
- Corporate governance
- Executive legal briefing
- Incident response and practical decision-making

# Approach 

For each scenario, the analysis considers:

Legal issue → Commercial impact → Available options → Risk mitigation → Recommended action

The project is an independent educational exercise and does not constitute legal advice.
